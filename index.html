Keyboard

Capture mode holds the keys that normally throw you out of the page — Windows, Alt+Tab, Escape, the F-row. Hold Escape for one second to leave.

  1. 1Pick the layout that matches the board

Caps, Num and Scroll Lock cannot be held inside the page — the keyboard flips them before any website sees the key. So they are graded on what matters: the lock has to change state, not just send a keypress. One that reports a press but never moves the light is dead. Anything left on is called out in the verdict.

Capture mode needs Chrome, Edge or Opera for the full key hold. In Firefox and Safari the page still reads every key, but the operating system may take Windows/Command and some F-keys.

    What the computer cannot check

    • engravingInside the case lid, under a loupe. Genuine text is crisp and evenly spaced. Fakes are dotty, shallow or slightly crooked.
    • hingeMetal, firm, and it stops cleanly. A loose or plasticky hinge with a soft stop is the single fastest tell.
    • weightWeigh your sealed reference pair and its case, write the numbers on the bench. Counterfeits are almost always lighter — compare against your own known-good figures, not against numbers off the internet.
    • meshThe speaker and microphone grilles are a fine, even mesh set flush. Fakes use coarser mesh, often sitting slightly proud or recessed.
    • magnetsBuds snap into the case decisively and the lid pulls itself shut. Weak or vague magnets mean cheap ones.
    • pairingOn an iPhone, a genuine pair raises the full pairing card with the correct model name and an animation. Fakes often show a generic card, the wrong name, or nothing at all.
    • squeezeOn Pro models the stem squeeze gives a distinct click through the bud. Touch-only controls with no click are a strong tell.

    How to weigh it all up

    No single check here is conclusive, including the sweep. Faults cluster, and so do fakes.

    • send backDriver response more than 6 dB out, and ear detection does nothing, and the hinge feels wrong. Three independent signals agreeing is as close to certain as you will get without Apple.
    • look againOne signal fails and the rest pass. Re-seat the bud and sweep again, check the ear-detection setting is actually on, and look at the engraving before you decide.
    • sell itResponse within 3 dB, ear detection pauses, serial not seen before, case and engraving clean, and both buds survive a dropout watch.

    Keep the sealed reference pair in a drawer and do not sell it. It is a measuring instrument now, and it is worth more to you than its resale value.

    GPU load

    Idle The shader draws here while the test runs.

    The picture is meaningless on purpose — an interference pattern recomputed for every pixel, every frame, to keep the graphics chip busy. What matters is that it keeps moving. Stutter, tearing or a freeze while the clock keeps climbing means the chip is unstable under load. Raise Graphics load if a machine sails through.

    How to read it

    • goodFrame rate holds flat for the whole run and the fans settle. Cooling is doing its job.
    • throttleFrame rate starts high then steps down after thirty to ninety seconds. That is thermal throttling — dust, dried paste, or a fan not spinning.
    • faultLong stalls, the image freezing, the tab crashing or the machine restarting. Power delivery, memory or the graphics chip.

    Browsers cannot read temperatures or fan speeds, so this measures the symptom rather than the cause. For actual sensor numbers you still need a tool on the machine itself.
